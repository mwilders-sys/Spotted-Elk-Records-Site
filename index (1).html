Who Was Chief Spotted Elk?What the Records Show

Family research and interpretation by

Ōpȟáŋ Glešká, known in English as Chief Spotted Elk, was a Minneconjou leader. He was killed at Wounded Knee in 1890.

Historical records also use the name Big Foot for an Oglala man. That is a different person. When two people show up in the records under the same English name, it's easy to mix them up.

This page puts the records side by side, so you can see which person each document is about, who his documented relatives are, and how public claims compare with the records.

This site is a work in progress. I update it as time allows. If you have information or documents to contribute, please send them to Michelle Wilder - Spotted Elk Tiwahe.

Lakota Kinship Clusters and Public Claims of Descent from Chief Spotted Elk

These charts trace relationships within and among families named in the records, including the Pipe on Head, Mousseau, He Crow, High Hawk, Little Finger, and Blue Legs families. The charts show marriages, stepfamily ties, shared households, and other relationships. Being included here does not establish direct descent from Chief Spotted Elk.
