Who Was Chief Spotted Elk?What the Records Show

Family research and interpretation by

Ōpȟáŋ Glešká (Chief Spotted Elk) was a Minneconjou Lakota leader who was later called Big Foot (Sitanka). He was killed at Wounded Knee in 1890.

An Oglala man was also known as Big Foot. This site brings together records that help identify each man, trace family relationships, and evaluate public claims of descent from Chief Spotted Elk.

This site is a work in progress. I update it as time allows. If you have information or documents to contribute, please send them to Michelle Wilder - Spotted Elk Tiwahe.
